**Description:** Orchestrator for the full academic research pipeline: research → write → integrity check → review → revise → re-review → re-revise → final integrity check → finalize. Coordinates [[deep-research]], [[academic-paper]], and [[academic-paper-reviewer]] into a 10-stage workflow with auditable QA artifacts.
