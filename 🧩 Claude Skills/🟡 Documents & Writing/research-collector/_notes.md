## research-collector

**Description:** Collects research material for an article/topic by gathering YouTube videos and web articles into a NotebookLM notebook, then runs analysis queries and saves results as markdown. Combines yt-dlp search, NotebookLM `nlm` CLI, and markdown reporting.
